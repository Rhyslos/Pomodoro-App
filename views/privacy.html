Privacy Policy

What Information We Collect

In accordance with data minimization principles, we collect only the information necessary to identify you on our system. This includes a display name, an irreversibly hashed password, and a temporary authentication token.

Telemetry & Public Contributions

We collect anonymous telemetry data regarding room usage, such as session duration, total tasks completed, and the maximum number of participants in a room. This data is strictly used to monitor platform health and is entirely decoupled from your personal identity.

Why We Collect It

Your account data is used exclusively to secure your identity, issue authentication tokens, and facilitate real-time collaboration in Pomodoro rooms.

Security Measures

Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are hashed using standard cryptographic protocols (SHA-256) before being saved. Sessions are managed statelessly via secure tokens. All data is encrypted in transit.

Your Rights & Deletion

You have the right to retract your consent and erase your personal data at any time. Clicking "Delete Account" permanently purges your display name, password hash, and active session tokens from our system. Anonymous telemetry data remains intact as it cannot be traced back to you.

Consent

By creating an account, you actively consent to the secure storage of your credentials and the collection of anonymous room metrics.
