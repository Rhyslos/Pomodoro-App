Privacy Policy

What Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of information necessary to provide the service. This includes a self-selected display name and an anonymous session identifier.

Telemetry & Analytics

We collect anonymous telemetry data regarding room usage, such as session duration, total number of rooms created, and the maximum number of participants in a room. This data is strictly used to monitor app usage trends and is completely decoupled from your personal identity. It cannot be traced back to you.

Why We Collect It

This data is used exclusively to facilitate real-time collaboration in Pomodoro rooms and to understand general platform usage.

Limitations on Storage

We do not use databases to persistently store your personal profile. Your display name and session ID are stored locally on your device. The server only holds this information in active memory while you are connected.

Security Measures

Because your personal data resides on your device, the risk of a centralized data breach exposing your identity is eliminated. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your Rights & Deletion

Because we do not store your data on our servers, you can exercise your right to be forgotten at any time by clicking "Delete Account" within the app, which erases your local data.

Consent

By entering a display name and joining a room, you consent to the temporary transmission of this name to our servers and the collection of anonymous room metrics.
